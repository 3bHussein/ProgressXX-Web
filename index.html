Gym & Nutrition Calculators

Evidence-based equations where possible; results are estimates, not medical advice.

🔥 Calories / TDEE

Mifflin–St Jeor BMR × activity factor, then goal adjustment.

Enter your details.
Nutrition sync
Uses the exact goal-adjusted Calories / TDEE result and the current Macro Targets settings. ProgressX saves the calculator profile, Nutrition targets and syncs them to Google Sheet.
Formula: Mifflin–St Jeor. Activity multipliers are population-level estimates.

🥩 Macro targets

Set calories, body weight and protein/fat targets; carbs fill the remaining calories.

Protein 1.6–2.2 g/kg is a common practical range for resistance training.

🏋️ 1RM estimator

Uses Epley, Brzycki and Lander, then shows their average. Best around 2–10 reps.

Enter a challenging set.

🧱 Plate calculator

Calculates plates per side for a barbell. Available plates can be customized.

Uses matching pairs, one plate on each side.

📊 Training volume

Volume load = sets × reps × external load. Compare like-for-like exercises and setups.

Enter a set scheme.

📈 Progressive overload

Simple double-progression helper: hit the top of your rep range across all working sets before adding load.

Log all working-set reps for a suggestion.

💧 Hydration target

Starting estimate based on body mass, plus training-time fluid. Climate and sweat rate can change needs substantially.

A practical starting estimate, not a rigid prescription.

⚡ Creatine guide

Maintenance target from body weight; loading is optional.

Typical maintenance is about 3–5 g/day.

📐 BMI

Quick screening metric. It does not distinguish muscle from fat mass.

Use alongside waist, body composition and performance context.

🧍 Navy body-fat estimate

Circumference-based estimate. Measure with a tape under consistent conditions.

Treat the result as a trend estimate, not a lab measurement.
